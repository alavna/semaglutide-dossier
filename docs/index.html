01

Executive summary

The current evidence, in one view

Semaglutide is a long-acting GLP-1 receptor agonist with strong randomized-trial and regulatory evidence for glycemic control in type 2 diabetes, substantial weight loss in selected overweight or obesity populations, and reduced major cardiovascular events in high-risk populations. Its established mechanisms include glucose-dependent insulin stimulation, glucagon suppression, delayed gastric emptying and appetite reduction. Common gastrointestinal adverse effects and several uncommon or uncertain risks require individualized monitoring. Evidence for adipose browning, mitochondrial, neuroprotective, Alzheimer’s disease and PCOS benefits remains predominantly preclinical or otherwise insufficient. Important unresolved issues include long-term safety, subgroup risks, treatment durability, access inequities and limited direct evidence from affected communities.
